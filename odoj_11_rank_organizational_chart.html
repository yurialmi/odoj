Civ Ops Lane

Field Inspections & Audits

  • Conducts undercover business audits & site inspections.
  • Collects physical receipt evidence and consumer complaints.
  • No DOJ Overlap: ODOJ does not employ field investigators.

Comptroller Lane

Treasury & Accounting Records

  • Manages state treasury ledger and tax collection logs.
  • Flags delinquent business taxes & unpaid state fees.
  • No DOJ Overlap: ODOJ does not maintain state financial ledgers.

ODOJ Enforcement Lane

Civil Lawsuits & Prosecution

  • Receives case files referred from Civ Ops or Comptroller.
  • Files civil lawsuits for debt recovery or agency defense in court.
  • Appoints judges via government budget allocations.

Standard Referral & Trial Handoff Workflow

Step 1 • Audit / Intake
Evidence Gathering

Civ Ops audits business or Comptroller identifies unpaid debt/taxes.

Step 2 • ODOJ Intake
Deputy AG Case Intake

Deputy AG assigns case packet to Civil Litigation or Economic Enforcement.

Step 3 • Filing
Clerk Docketing

Legal Clerks draft civil summons and schedule hearing on court docket.

Step 4 • Trial
Judicial Hearing

Appointed Judge presides independently; renders civil judgment/penalty.
